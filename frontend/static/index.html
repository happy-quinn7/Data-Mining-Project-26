Welcome

In this study you chat with an AI assistant while we record how you type. Talk about anything you like, the way you would with ChatGPT.

  • What is recorded: the keys you press in the message box, the characters they produce, and when each key goes down and up.
  • Nothing else on your computer is recorded.
  • The recording is stored under a code like P04, never your name.
  • It takes about 15 minutes. Type naturally: speed and typos don't matter.
